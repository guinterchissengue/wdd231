Student Success Hub Site Plan

A website designed to help students improve their study habits, manage their time effectively, and access useful academic resources.

Site Name

Student Success Hub

This name was selected because the website acts as a central hub where students can find study resources, productivity tips, and academic support in one place.

Optional Domain: studentsuccesshub.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide students with practical academic support and learning resources. It will include study tips, exam preparation strategies, time management techniques, productivity advice, motivational content, and recommended educational resources.

Scenarios

  • How can I prepare effectively for exams with limited time?
  • What are the best ways to manage assignments and deadlines?
  • Where can I find reliable study resources for difficult subjects?
  • How can I stay motivated during stressful school periods?

Color Scheme

Primary Color: #1F5F8B

Used for headings, navigation, and main UI elements.

Accent Color: #C76E00

Used for buttons, highlights, and important elements.

Secondary Color: #F6F5F5

Used for backgrounds and content sections.

Typography

Poppins is used for headings and titles.

Open Sans is used for body text and general content.
