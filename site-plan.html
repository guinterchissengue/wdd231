Student Success Hub Site Plan

A website designed to help students improve their study habits, manage their time effectively, and access useful academic resources.

Site Name

Student Success Hub

This name was selected because the website acts as a central hub where students can find study resources, productivity tips, academic support, and motivational content in one place.

Optional Domain: studentsuccesshub.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide students with practical academic support and learning resources. The site will include study tips, assignment help, exam preparation advice, time management strategies, productivity techniques, motivational content, and recommended educational resources. The goal is to help students stay organized, improve their study habits, and achieve academic success.

Scenarios

  • How can I prepare effectively for an upcoming exam when I have limited study time?
  • What are the best time management strategies for balancing assignments and classes?
  • Where can I find reliable online resources to better understand difficult subjects?
  • How can I stay motivated and productive throughout the semester?

Color Schema

Primary Color: #2E86AB

This color will be used for page headings, navigation areas, banners, and other important sections.

Accent Color: #F18F01

This color will be used for buttons, highlights, important links, and calls to action.

Secondary Color: #F6F5F5

This color will be used for page backgrounds and content sections.

Typography

Poppins will be used for headings, titles, and navigation elements because it provides a modern and professional appearance.

Open Sans will be used for body text, paragraphs, and general content because it is easy to read on different screen sizes.
